## Knowledge Base (KB) Self-Service Guides

### KB-001: How to Fix Wi-Fi & VPN Drops on Windows 10/11

#### Overview
If your laptop shows *"No Internet, Secured"* or your company VPN keeps disconnecting while working from the office or home, follow these quick self-service steps before raising an IT ticket.

#### Step-by-Step Troubleshooting
1. **Toggle Your Wi-Fi Off and On:** Click the Wi-Fi/Network icon at the bottom-right corner of your taskbar, turn Wi-Fi **Off**, wait 10 seconds, and turn it back **On**.
2. **Disconnect and Reconnect Your VPN:** Open your VPN client, click **Disconnect**, wait 5 seconds, and click **Connect** again.
3. **Check Your Browser Connection:** Open your web browser and try loading a standard webpage (such as `google.com`) to confirm your internet connection is active before launching the VPN.
4. **Restart Your Laptop:** If the connection still drops, save your work and restart your computer to clear temporary network glitches.

#### Still Need Help?
If you are still unable to connect after trying these steps, please raise a ticket under **Report a system problem** and mention your floor or location so the IT Service Desk can assist you.

### KB-002: Self-Service Guide: How to Clear Print Spooler & Fix Offline Printers

#### Overview
When a network printer shows an **"Offline"** status or documents get stuck in the print queue, restarting the Windows Print Spooler service usually fixes the issue within one minute.

#### Step-by-Step Troubleshooting
1. **Check Printer Power and Network:** Make sure the printer is turned on, has paper loaded, and shows no blinking error lights on its display screen.
2. **Open Windows Services:** Press the **Windows Key + R** on your keyboard, type `services.msc` in the box, and press **Enter**.
3. **Restart the Print Spooler:** Scroll down the alphabetical list, find **Print Spooler**, right-click on it, and select **Restart**.
4. **Retry Printing:** Wait 10 seconds, open your document, and send your print job again.

#### Still Need Help?
If the printer still shows as offline or displays a hardware error code, please submit a support request with the **Printer Name/Asset Tag** and your **Office Floor** so a technician can inspect it.
